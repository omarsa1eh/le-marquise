Common Questions

Quick answers about working with La Marquise Group

Where is La Marquise Group based?

La Marquise Group is headquartered in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates and provides facility management and real estate services across the UAE.

What facility management services do you offer in Abu Dhabi?

We deliver integrated facility management including MEP maintenance, HVAC, cleaning, swimming pool care, fire safety, landscaping, interior design and general contracting. Annual Maintenance Contracts are available for commercial, residential and recreational facilities.

Does La Marquise work with Abu Dhabi Civil Defence requirements?

Yes. Our fire and life safety maintenance complies with Abu Dhabi Civil Defence (ADCD) regulations and the UAE Fire & Life Safety Code, including periodic inspection, testing and emergency response.

How do I request a facility management quotation?

Use the contact form on marquise.ae or email info.fm@marquise.ae. Our team responds within one business day with a site survey or proposal.
