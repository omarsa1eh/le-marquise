MEP Engineer

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Mechanical or Electrical Engineering
  • Minimum 5 years of MEP experience in facilities management or construction
  • Strong knowledge of MEP design, installation, and maintenance practices
  • Hands-on experience with chiller systems (maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair)
  • Familiarity with UAE codes, AutoCAD, and Revit MEP
  • Proven ability to coordinate multi-trade works on site
  • Must hold valid TAQA approval for electrical works in Abu Dhabi
  • Valid UAE driving license

Swimming Pool Technician

Requirements:

  • Minimum 3 years of hands-on swimming pool maintenance experience (commercial or competition-grade pools preferred)
  • Practical knowledge of water chemistry, sand-filter systems, circulation pumps, and chemical dosing
  • Ability to backwash filters, monitor pool plant equipment, and log daily readings
  • Familiarity with UAE pool-water quality and hygiene standards
  • Comfortable working with chemicals and following all PPE / HSE protocols
  • Pool operator certification (CPO, NSPF, or equivalent) is an advantage
